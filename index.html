I'm an MSc student in Information Retrieval and Knowledge Discovery at Sofia University, where I also work as a part-time research assistant on the SUMMIT project. My research centers on the reliability of LLM-as-a-judge systems — how to evaluate open-ended model outputs correctly, why judges fail, and how to build benchmarks and metrics that expose those failure modes. I recently interned at Google's Search team in Zurich, where I worked on citation-bias evaluation for AI Mode, and at Uber in Amsterdam, where I built observability tooling in Go. I've also been a teaching assistant at Sofia University since 2022, leading courses in Data Structures and Algorithms and Artificial Intelligence.
